# Bundled agent-template asset licenses

The following small glTF fixtures were authored for scena and are distributed
under the repository's `MIT OR Apache-2.0` license:

- `material_variants_scene.gltf`
- `animated_triangle_scene.glb`
- `cad_plate_drawing_scene.gltf`

They are embedded only to make installed `scena examples agent` workflows
portable; they do not contain Khronos sample-model files or third-party image
assets.
