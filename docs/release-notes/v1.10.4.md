# scena 1.10.4

Orthographic camera framing for authored CAD and technical-view recipes.

- Accept orthographic cameras as authored recipe framing targets.
- Fit orthographic horizontal and vertical extents to requested scene bounds
  while preserving the configured viewport aspect ratio.
- Derive the orthographic near and far range from the framed depth bounds so
  technical views do not clip the requested model.
- Keep perspective framing behavior unchanged and cover both camera types with
  deterministic scene and recipe contract tests.
